Te Wa Privacy Policy

Te Wa – Kiribati Canoe Fishing · Last updated 1 October 2026

Summary

Te Wa does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few settings only on your own phone or computer, using the browser's local storage:

  • your best score, game coins and the gear you own
  • which creatures you have found in the Fish Book
  • the player names you typed for multiplayer, and your language, age level and sound settings

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app or clearing the browser's site data removes it.

Network requests

The game files are loaded from GitHub Pages, and the fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address in order to deliver the files. Te Wa does not send them any information about you or how you play. After the first visit, the game works offline.

Children

Te Wa is made for children and students. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children. Game coins are for play only: they cannot be bought or exchanged for real money.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project page: github.com/teetamaribo7-cpu/game.
