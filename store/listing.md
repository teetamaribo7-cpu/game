## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Mauri! Paddle your canoe across the lagoons of Kiribati, drop your line and bring home the best catch before the sun sets.

Te Wa is a fishing game for children and families, made for Kiribati and the Pacific. It is fun to play and teaches about Kiribati, the ocean and money skills along the way.

CATCH REAL PACIFIC FISH
• Milkfish (te baneawa), skipjack tuna (te ati), flying fish (te onauti), bonefish (te ikari), giant trevally, yellowfin tuna and mahi-mahi
• Watch out for blacktip reef sharks (te bakoa). They cut your line and steal your fish!
• Sea turtles (te on) are protected, so let them swim free
• Hook plastic rubbish to clean the lagoon

BUILD YOUR FISHING CAREER
• Sell your catch at the fish market by weight (kg × price)
• Save your coins for stronger rods to land bigger fish
• Upgrade from a paddle canoe to a sailing canoe and a fibreglass boat with an outboard motor
• Fish three grounds: the Lagoon, the Reef edge and the Open ocean
• Outboard motors need fuel, so learn that profit = sales − costs

LEARN WHILE YOU PLAY
• Fish Book: discover 11 sea creatures, each with a card of simple facts
• Bonus quiz after every trip about Kiribati, ocean life, the environment, Kiribati words and maths
• Two age levels: 6–9 and 10–14
• Practice quiz for the classroom

PLAY TOGETHER
• 1 player: fish until sunset and beat your best catch
• 2 players on one screen: red canoe against blue canoe
• Pass & Play: 2 to 4 players take turns on one phone

MADE FOR KIRIBATI
• Works offline, with no data needed after the first time you open it
• Small download that runs well on low-cost phones
• English and te taetae ni Kiribati
• Outrigger canoes, coconut palms, the frigatebird (te eitei) and a Kiribati flag sail

No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. No personal data collected.
Game coins are for play only and cannot be bought with real money.

Ko rabwa!
